Your clipboard, as a story

Every copy is a clue. Clip Story links them into what you were actually doing, flags what you left unfinished, and writes it up each week.

No report yet. Copy things for a while, then press "Write this week".

Timeline

Copies grouped into work sessions (a gap of 10 minutes starts a new one). Coloured bars mark copies the AI linked into one thread.

Vault

Passwords, keys and card numbers stay one click away: here, in the popup, and on the login page itself. On ARM they are stored only encrypted, and the AI only ever sees "[password, 14 chars] on gmail.com".

Settings

Clip Story keeps a local copy for 30 days and the full history on your ARM server, forever.

ARM connection
The server listens only on ARM's loopback. Your laptop reaches it through an SSH tunnel:
ssh -N -L 8790:127.0.0.1:8790 Hunter-A1-Flex
The Windows logon task in AI automation\clip_story\tunnel\ keeps this running.
